## Game Concept (Snake)

The goal of the snake is to eat as many foods as possible in order to grow and earn points. The snake gets long and because of this, it can get harder and harder to move. The player must avoid the snake touching or colliding with its body in order not to lose. When the snake goes over the map, its head will pass through the opposite side of the world.

<b>RULES:</b><br>
* The snake only moves `UP`, `DOWN`, `LEFT`, and `RIGHT`
* When the snake eats food, it must get longer. New tail is added
* When the snake's head hit its body, <b>GAME OVER</b>.
* Food only spawns when the last one was eaten
* When the snake goes beyond the world, it must show up on the opposite side.
